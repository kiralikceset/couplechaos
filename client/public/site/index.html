How it works

Three steps. Zero talent needed.

Give hints

One of you sees a secret word and sends hints. Saying the word itself is not allowed.

Draw and guess

The other one draws from the hints, live, then picks the answer out of four options.

Get judged

An AI scores the drawing out of 100 and roasts it. Then you swap roles and do it again.

Questions

Is it free?

Yes. Couple Chaos is free to play with no ads, with 5 games a day. Premium unlocks unlimited games and every theme.

Do we both need the app?

No. One of you sends a link and the other can play straight from the browser on any phone.

Can we play in the same room on one phone?

Each of you needs a screen, since one person must not see the secret word. Two phones, or one phone and a laptop, works best.

Which languages?

English, Turkish, German and Spanish.

What happens to our drawings?

Your game history stays on your phone. A finished drawing is sent to an AI service only to score it, without your name or email. Details are in the Privacy Policy.

How do I delete my account?

In the app: Profile, then “Delete account & data”. More in Account deletion.
