# Couple Chaos: launch kit

Everything needed to fill the store listings and plan the first month. Character limits are checked (see the table at the end).

## 1. Positioning (product-marketing)

- **What it is:** a two-player drawing and guessing game for couples. One person describes a secret word without saying it, the other draws and guesses, and an AI judge scores and roasts the drawing.
- **For whom:** couples aged 18–35, in the same room or long distance, who want a quick laugh together instead of scrolling side by side.
- **The problem it solves:** "what do we do tonight?" Board games need setup, quizzes get serious, and most party games need 4+ people.
- **Why it's different:**
  1. Built for exactly two people (most drawing games need a group).
  2. The AI judge: a score and a funny comment on every drawing, plus a side-by-side "your drawing vs the real thing".
  3. Rounds last under two minutes, playable on one invite link, no setup.
  4. A shared daily word with a leaderboard, so there's a reason to come back tomorrow.
- **Tone:** playful and a bit teasing, never cringe. Short sentences. The jokes are about the drawings, not about the relationship.
- **One-line pitch:** "Draw it, guess it, get roasted. A 2-minute drawing game for the two of you."

## 5. Pricing and paywall (pricing, paywalls, marketing-psychology)

- **Model:** freemium. 5 free games a day (already in code: `FREE_DAILY_LIMIT`), Premium subscription, token packs for hints.
- **Suggested prices (USD base, let Play auto-convert, then lower TR by hand):**
  - Premium monthly $3.99, yearly $24.99 (shown as "$2.08/month, save 48%"), 3-day free trial on yearly only.
  - Tokens: 60 for $0.99, 200 for $2.99 ("popular"), 500 for $5.99 ("best value").
  - Turkey: monthly ₺79.99, yearly ₺449.99. Couples app pricing in TR sits well below USD conversion.
- **Paywall moments (in order of intent):** after the 5th free game of the day; when picking a locked theme; from the crown on Home. Never mid-round.
- **Paywall copy rules:** lead with the outcome ("Unlimited nights of chaos"), show yearly first and pre-selected, show the trial timeline (already built), one clear button, "Restore purchases" and terms links visible.
- **One subscription covers both partners?** Not today. It's the most-asked feature in couple apps; consider "Premium for two" (one purchase unlocks the partner's room) as the first pricing experiment after launch.

## 6. Onboarding and sign-up (onboarding, signup)

- Current flow: intro animation (first launch only) → 3 how-it-works slides → sign-in (Apple / Google / email). Good: value before the wall.
- Activation goal: **first finished round within 3 minutes of install.** After sign-in, go straight to "Invite your partner" with "Play with AI" as the fallback, so a solo first-timer still finishes a round.
- Measure later (needs analytics, which the privacy policy currently says we don't use; decide before adding any): install → sign-in → first round → second-day return.

## 7. Launch plan (launch)

**Week −2 (now):** daily videos (2/day), bio link to the site, collect a waitlist? Not needed: the store page is the waitlist once published as "pre-registration" on Play.

**Week −1:** Play closed test with 12+ testers for 14 days (required for new personal developer accounts before production). Ask friends + followers; give them the invite link.

**Launch day:**
- Post 3 videos (morning/18:30/21:30) with "out now" and the store link in bio.
- Ask the first 20 testers for an honest review in the first 48 hours.
- Reply to every review within a day.

**Weeks 1–4:**
- Switch content from informational to POV/couple sketches (plan in PLAN.md).
- Daily word on social: post the day's word drawing as a story ("can you guess it?").
- Track: installs/day, D1 retention, free → paid conversion. Target for $5k/month: about 1,700 yearly-equivalent subscribers, so conversion and retention matter more than raw installs.
